📖 DFFL Rules & Scoring

Eight owners. Eight NFL divisions. One 8-player lineup each week. Owners build rosters only from the NFL teams inside the division they control.

🏈

Unlike traditional fantasy football, DFFL owners draft an NFL division rather than individual players.

Advance Roster Submissions

Owners may submit lineups for future weeks at any time as a backup for travel, work, vacations, or simply to avoid missing a deadline. The most recent valid submission before Thursday at noon Central Time becomes the official roster for that week.

🚑
Late Injury Replacement Rule

If a player on an owner’s official DFFL roster is ruled out, injured, inactive, or otherwise unable to play after the Thursday 12:00 PM Central roster deadline, the owner may request a replacement.

To make the change official:

  1. The owner must submit the roster again with the injured/inactive player still listed in the original roster spot.
  2. In the additional submission/message field, the owner must clearly identify:
    • The injured or inactive player being replaced.
    • The replacement player who should be used instead.
  3. The replacement request must be submitted at least 5 minutes before the scheduled kickoff of that injured player’s NFL game.
  4. The replacement player must still satisfy all normal DFFL roster rules, including position eligibility, division eligibility, duplicate-player rules, and the applicable 4-, 3-, or 2-active-team roster requirements.
  5. Once the affected NFL game begins, no replacement may be made for that player.

Example

An owner submitted Chris Olave in their official Thursday roster. On Sunday morning, Olave is ruled out.

The owner should resubmit the roster with Chris Olave still shown in the official WR spot, then use the replacement notification to state:

Chris Olave OUT — replace with Rashid Shaheed.

That replacement submission must be received at least 5 minutes before the scheduled kickoff of the Saints game.

This rule is intended only for players who become unavailable after the normal weekly roster deadline and does not reopen the entire roster for general lineup changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common league questions.

Can I submit future lineups?

Yes. Owners may submit future weeks in advance and update them until Thursday at 12:00 PM Central Time for that week.

What happens if I miss the deadline?

There are no point penalties. Only a valid roster submitted by the weekly deadline can become the official lineup for that week.

What if one of my players is ruled out after Thursday's deadline?

You may use the Late Injury Replacement Rule. Resubmit the roster with the injured or inactive player still in the original roster spot, identify the replacement in the additional submission/message field, and submit the request at least 5 minutes before that player’s scheduled NFL kickoff.

How do bye weeks work?

Roster balance automatically changes based on whether 4, 3, or 2 NFL teams in your division are active.

Can I use the same player every week?

Yes, as long as the player remains eligible within your division and the weekly roster satisfies all lineup and team-balance rules.

How are standings ties broken?

Head-to-head, divisional record, point differential, total fantasy points scored, then coin flip.
