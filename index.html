🏈 DFFL Roster Requirements

Every weekly lineup must contain exactly 8 players. The team-balance rule changes based on how many teams in your NFL division are active that week.

🟢 4 Active Teams — Normal Week
  • Minimum 1 player from each NFL team.
  • Maximum 3 players from any one NFL team.

Valid example: 3 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 8

🟡 3 Active Teams — One Team on Bye
  • Minimum 2 players from each active NFL team.
  • Maximum 4 players from any one active NFL team.

Valid example: 4 + 2 + 2 = 8

🔴 2 Active Teams — Two Teams on Bye
  • Exactly 4 players from each active NFL team.

Required: 4 + 4 = 8

Bye-week rules are based on the active NFL teams available in your selected division for that week.
